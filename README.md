## About Me

I'm **Guilherme**, an 18-year-old engineering student at **Instituto Superior Técnico (IST)**, with a strong passion for games, programming, and building creative ideas.

I'm ambitious by nature and constantly sketching new concepts, systems, and projects — although, let's be honest, many of them still live happily in the design phase, waiting for "the right moment" to finally be built.

My programming background spans **Python, C, Prolog** (yes, old-school YAP Prolog), and **Assembly**, and I'm continuously expanding my toolbox with new languages.

## Current Focus

- Building a personal **Python-based productivity system**, using custom terminal commands — long-term goal: evolve it into a self-built AI assistant for organization and automation.
- Studying **Computer Engineering** at IST.
- Open to collaborating on ambitious projects with real impact.
- Happy to talk tech, ideas, personal interests, and long-term goals.
- Reach me through my socials below.

## Fun Facts

- I don't drink coffee. At all. My code runs on stubbornness alone.
- I enjoy exploring multiple hobbies and picking up new skills.
- When I commit to something, I stay locked in until it's finished — pun on "commit" fully intended, see below.
